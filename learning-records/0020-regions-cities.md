# 0020 — Karnataka regions & cities

## Date
2026-08-06

## Decision
After L2 “where going” and L19 food/place culture, teach hometown small talk: **ooru**, **Neevu ellinda?**, **Nanna ooru X**, **X-inda**, plus high-frequency official city names (Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangaluru, Udupi, Hubballi, Belagavi, Kalaburagi, Kodagu) and spoken region chips (karavali, malenadu, uttara Karnataka). Includes cartoon enactment.

## Consequences
Neighbour identity talk is unblocked. Future travel / bus-station lessons can reuse city names without re-teaching the ooru/ellinda frame. Not a full 31-district civics lesson.
