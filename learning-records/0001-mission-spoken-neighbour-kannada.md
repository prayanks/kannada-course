# 0001 — Mission locked: spoken neighbour Kannada

## Date
2026-07-15 (mission); Lesson 1 authored 2026-07-25

## Context
Learner is A0 with only *Namaskara*. Lives in Bengaluru; wants speak+understand only, Roman transliteration, Hindi parallels.

## Decision
Lesson 1 focuses on **polite neighbour greeting + check-in**, not numbers/shopping yet. Polite *neevu / nimma* register is the first grammar lever because society life is mostly polite-plural.

## Consequences
- Next lesson should either deepen the same exchange (time-of-day, “where are you going?”) or pivot to *Eshtu?* (prices) after L1 is solid in real life.
- Script stays out of scope until conversational base exists.
