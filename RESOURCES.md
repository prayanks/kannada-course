# Resources

## Knowledge

### Spoken / beginner Kannada (grounding)
- [WikiBooks: Kannada — Conversational phrases](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Kannada/Conversational_Phrases) — basic phrase inventory; verify against Bengaluru speech.
- [Omniglot: Kannada phrases](https://www.omniglot.com/language/phrases/kannada.php) — quick phrase list with romanisation.
- [Kannada Kali / community phrase notes](https://kannadakali.com/) — learner-oriented spoken material (spot-check formality).

### Grammar parallels (Hindi bridge)
- Treat SOV order and T–V politeness (*tu / aap* ↔ *neenu / neevu*) as the bridge from Hindi — not a substitute for listening to locals.

## Wisdom (practice communities)
- Speak with neighbours / society staff (primary lab)
- Local language-exchange or Bengaluru expat/learner groups (optional)
