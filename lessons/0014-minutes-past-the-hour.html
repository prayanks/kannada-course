Lesson 0014 Spoken Kannada ~11 min

Minutes past the hour

Move beyond whole hours: quarter, half, and “N minutes” so meeting times and train slots don’t force you into English only.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Say and catch Aidu gante kaalu · Aidu gante ardha · Aidu gante hattu nimisha · Aidu gante-ge munche / nantara.

Spaced review: Eshtu gante aytu? · Aidu gante aytu · Ardha · Eega · Naale hattu gante-ge · Nimisha

01 Why this matters

L9 locked whole hours. Real life is 10:15, 7:30, 5 minutes late. Bengaluru mixes English clock times with Kannada glue — learn the Kannada spine so you hear both.

02 One lever: gante + fraction / nimisha

Pattern

Aidu gante kaalu ≈ 5:15 (quarter past)
Aidu gante ardha ≈ 5:30 (you know ardha)
Aidu gante muvattu nimisha ≈ 5:30 (literal minutes — also heard)
Aidu gante-ge hattu nimisha late ≈ 10 minutes late for 5:00

Kaalu = quarter (literally “leg/quarter”). Very common for :15. For :45 many people say English “quarter to” or next hour-ge munche kaalu — we’ll stay practical.

04 Phrase cards

Aidu gante kaalu

slownatural

Quarter past five (5:15)

≈ Paanch savva / paanch-pandrah

kaalu = quarter-hour. Works with any hour you know.

Aidu gante ardha

slownatural

Half past five (5:30)

≈ Saadhe paanch

Recycle L9. Prefer this over long minute counts.

Hattu gante ippatthu nimisha

slownatural

Ten twenty (10:20)

≈ Das-bees

Pattern: HOUR gante + MINUTES nimisha. Use L3/L8 numbers.

Elu gante-ge munche

slownatural

Before seven

≈ Saat se pehle

munche = before. Pair with appointments.

Elu gante-ge nantara · Elu gante aaytu

slownatural
slownatural

After seven · Seven has struck / it’s past seven

≈ Saat ke baad · Saat baj gaye

nantara (L4). aaytu related to aytu — time has come/passed.

Swalpa late aytu · Aidu nimisha late

slownatural
slownatural

A bit late · Five minutes late

≈ Thoda late · Paanch minute late

Compose L9 late + numbers + nimisha.

06 Model dialogues

A · Meeting time

Colleague
Yaavaaga meeting?

slownatural

When is the meeting?

You
Hattu gante kaalu-ge.

slownatural

At 10:15.

Colleague
Eega eshtu gante?

slownatural

What time is it now?

You
Hattu gante. Innu hattu nimisha ide.

slownatural

Ten. Still ten minutes left.

B · Running late

You
Swalpa late aytu — aidu nimisha.

slownatural

A bit late — five minutes.

Other
Sari sari. Elu gante ardha-ge banni.

slownatural

No problem. Come at 7:30.

You
Sari. Gothaytu.

slownatural

Okay. Got it.

09 Real-world task

Before next lesson
  1. Look at a real clock three times today; say the time with kaalu / ardha / N nimisha.
  2. Schedule one thing out loud: … gante kaalu-ge or … ardha-ge.
  3. If you’re late once, say Swalpa late — aidu nimisha (even to yourself first).
