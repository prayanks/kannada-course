Bank, ATM & UPI

Find an ATM, ask for cash, and pay at a shop counter with UPI, card, or cash — Bengaluru’s real money mix.

01 Why this matters

After hotel desk talk (L21), the next freeze point is money on the street: finding an ATM, asking for cash, and choosing UPI / card / cash at a shop. Bengaluru speech is heavy Kannada–English mix here — ATM, UPI, cash, card, QR, change, chiller sit inside Kannada frames like elli ide?, ideya?, maadi, and tagontira?.

02 One lever: place + pay-method frames

Pattern

X elli ide? = where is X? (ATM / bank)
X ideya? = is X available? (UPI / cash)
X maadi = please do X (UPI maadi · QR scan maadi)
X tagontira? = do you take / accept X? (card / UPI)

Hindi bridge: ATM kahan hai? → ATM elli ide? · UPI kar digiye → UPI maadi · Card lete ho? → Card tagontira?.

03 Money board (say these out loud)

ATM bank duddu rokka cash UPI card QR scan change chiller tagontira

Duddu / rokka = money (Kannada). Cash is the street loan for notes. Chiller = small change / loose notes (Bengaluru English inside KN talk). Tagontira? = do you take/accept?

07 Model dialogue — shop counter (cash short → UPI)

Shop
Eshtu aytu? Nooru aivattu.

slownatural

You
Kshamisi. Nanage cash kammi. UPI ideya?

slownatural

Sorry. I’m short on cash. Is UPI available?

Shop
Haudu. QR scan maadi.

slownatural

Yes. Please scan the QR.

You
Sari. UPI maadtidini… Payment aytu.

slownatural

Okay. I’m doing UPI… Payment done.

Shop

slownatural

You

slownatural

2. “I need cash”

4. “Do you take card?”
5. “Please give change”
6. Spaced (L21): “Is there a vacant room?”

09 Production

  1. Where is the ATM / bank?
  2. I need cash. Fifty thousand.
  3. Is UPI available? Please do UPI.
  4. Do you take card? Cash or UPI?
  5. Please scan the QR. Payment done.
  6. Please give change / I need chiller.

Cover English; speak out loud. If the cashier is fast: Matte heli / Nidhana heli (L18).
