Lesson 0017 Spoken Kannada ~12 min

Pharmacy & not feeling well

Tell a chemist what’s wrong, ask for medicine, confirm how to take it — high-stakes, short phrases.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Run: Nanage fever ide · Cold / cough / pain · Medicine beku · After food · Eshtu aytu?

Spaced review: Dayavittu · Matte heli · Idhu eshtu? · Ondu packet · Bill kodri · Nanage …

01 Why this matters

Shops and autos are frequent; pharmacy is rarer but important. Staff often start in Kannada. A calm symptom + medicine script beats panicked English-only.

02 One lever: Nanage … ide (I have …)

Pattern

Nanage fever ide ≈ I have fever
Nanage headache ide ≈ I have a headache
Medicine beku ≈ I need medicine
Body/symptom words often stay English in Bengaluru — that’s fine and natural.

05 Model dialogue

You
Namaskara. Dayavittu — nanage fever ide, cold ide.

slownatural

Hello. Please — I have fever and cold.

Chemist
Doctor nodidira?

slownatural

Did you see a doctor?

You
Illave. Medicine beku — swalpa soft.

slownatural

Not yet. Need medicine — a bit mild.

Chemist
Idhu tablet. After food, eradu times.

slownatural

This tablet. After food, two times.

You
Heghe tagobeku? Ondu tablet na?

slownatural

How to take? One tablet?

Chemist
Haudu. Neeru jasti kudi. Rest maadi.

slownatural

Yes. Drink more water. Rest.

You
Sari. Eshtu aytu?

slownatural

Okay. How much?

Chemist
Aivattu rupayi.

slownatural

Fifty rupees.

You
Sari. Dhanyavadagalu.

slownatural

Okay. Thank you.

08 Real-world task

Practice (even if healthy)

  3. Always confirm Heghe tagobeku? before leaving.
