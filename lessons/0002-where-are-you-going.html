Lesson 0002 Spoken Kannada ~10 min

Where are you going?

Extend the neighbour hello into real small talk: ask where someone’s headed, answer about yourself, and close politely — still Roman only, polite register.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

After Namaskara + Hegiddiri?, you can run: Elli hogtiddiri? → Naanu manege / office-ge hogtidini → Sari, nodona → Olle dinagalu.

Spaced review (Lesson 1): Say out loud before scrolling — Namaskara · Hegiddiri? · Chennagidini · Neevu? · Nimma hesaru enu? · Nanna hesaru Prayank · Dhanyavadagalu · Sari

01 Why this matters

In a society corridor, the greeting alone feels stiff. Locals almost always add “where are you going?” — same social role as Hindi kahaan jaa rahe ho? Answer short, then exit cleanly. That’s fluent neighbour life.

02 One grammar lever: -ge = “to”

Hindi uses -ko / ki taraf for destination. Kannada stacks -ge (or -kke after some words) on the place:

Pattern

mane + ge → manege (to home)
office + ge → office-ge (to office) — English nouns are fine in Bengaluru speech
Verb bit you’ll hear: hogtidini (I’m going) · hogtiddiri (you’re going, polite)

Rough map to Hindi continuous: jaa raha/rahi hoon ≈ hogtidini; polite “you are going” ≈ hogtiddiri.

03 Phrase cards

Elli hogtiddiri?

slownatural

Where are you going? (polite)

≈ Aap kahaan jaa rahe hain?

Word-by-word: elli (where) · hogtiddiri (are-going-you, polite) · Also hear: Elli hogtaiddira? / Elli hogtiddeera? — same intent. Default: Elli hogtiddiri?

Naanu manege hogtidini

slownatural

I’m going home

≈ Main ghar jaa raha/rahi hoon

Word-by-word: naanu (I) · mane-ge (home-to) · hogtidini (am-going) · mane = home/house.

Office-ge hogtidini

slownatural

I’m going to the office

≈ Main office jaa raha/rahi hoon

Swap the place: market-ge, shop-ge, gym-ge — Bengaluru mixes English place names freely.

Kelasa-ge hogtidini

slownatural

I’m going to work

≈ Main kaam pe jaa raha/rahi hoon

kelasa = work / job. Slightly more “Kannada” than saying only office.

Illi / Alli

Here / There

≈ Yahaan / Wahaan

Useful fragments: Illi barri (come here, polite — later). For now, recognition only if you hear them.

Sari, nodona

slownatural

Okay, see you / we’ll see

≈ Theek, milte hain

nodona ≈ “let’s see / see you” — very common exit. Soft, friendly close after small talk.

Olle dinagalu

slownatural

Have a good day

≈ Accha din ho / have a nice day

olle = good · dinagalu = day(s). Polite exit line after nodona.

Hogtini, sari

slownatural

I’ll get going, okay

≈ Main chalta/chalti hoon, theek hai

Short leave-taking if you’re the one walking away. Pair with Namaskara or Dhanyavadagalu.

05 Model dialogue

You
Namaskara. Hegiddiri?

slownatural

Hello. How are you?

Neighbour

slownatural

I’m fine. And you?

You
Chennagidini. Elli hogtiddiri?

slownatural

I’m fine. Where are you going?

Neighbour

slownatural

To the market. You?

You

slownatural

I’m going to the office. Okay, see you. Have a good day.

06 Retrieval practice

Equal-length options where possible.

1. Polite “Where are you going?”
2. “I’m going home”
3. What does the ending -ge mainly mark here?
4. Friendly exit: “Okay, see you”
5. “Have a good day”
6. Spaced: polite “How are you?” (Lesson 1)

07 Production (cover + speak)

Without looking, say in Kannada:

  1. Hello. How are you?
  2. I’m fine. Where are you going?
  3. I’m going home.
  4. I’m going to the office.
  5. Okay, see you. Have a good day.

Miss any line? Repeat it twice out loud, then rerun the full set.

08 Real-world task

  3. Close with Sari, nodona (bonus: Olle dinagalu).
