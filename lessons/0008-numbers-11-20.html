Lesson 0008 Spoken Kannada ~12 min

Numbers 11–20 (+ money stretch)

Extend L3 so shop and auto prices above ten don’t go blank — lock 11–20, recycle tens, and hear money in the wild.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Count hannondu → ippatthu out loud, catch prices like hadinaru / hadinentu / ippatthu rupayi, and still run Idhu eshtu? without freezing.

Spaced review (1–10): ondu eradu mooru naalku aidu aaru elu entu ombattu hattu · Idhu eshtu? · Swalpa kammi maadi

01 Why this matters

Auto minimums, snacks, and society payments live in the teens and twenties. L3 gave you 1–10 and a few anchors; this lesson makes the teen band automatic so you can hear the bill.

02 One pattern: teens ≈ “hadi-” + digit

Pattern

Many teens start with a hadi- sound (think “ten-and…”): 11 hannondu · 12 hanneradu · 13 hadimooru · … · 18 hadinentu · 19 hattombattu · 20 ippatthu.

Don’t overfit the rule — drill the list as chunks. Hindi bridge: 11–19 feel like gyarah–unnees inventory, not math.

04 Phrase cards (numbers in use)

Hadinaidu rupayi

slownatural

Fifteen rupees

≈ Pandrah rupaye

Number + rupayi (L3). Swap any teen.

Ippatthu rupayi sari na?

slownatural

Twenty rupees, okay?

≈ Bees rupaye, theek hai?

Confirm a quoted price. na? tag from L4.

Hanneradu idli · Hadimooru coffee

slownatural
slownatural

Twelve idlis · Thirteen coffees (party order energy)

≈ Barah idli…

Number before item works; for 1–2 you already use ondu/eradu after or before — both heard.

Eshtu? — Hadinaru.

slownatural

How much? — Sixteen.

≈ Kitna? — Solah.

Train your ear: answer may be bare number, no “rupayi.”

Ippatthu-ge swalpa kammi maadi

slownatural

Please reduce a bit from twenty

≈ Bees se thoda kam

Compose L3 bargain + new number. -ge can mark the amount you’re pushing on.

Nooru alla — aivattu

slownatural

Not hundred — fifty

≈ Sau nahi — pachaas

alla ≈ not (negation particle). Useful when you misheard.

08 Production

  1. Count 11→20 slowly, then 20→11.
  2. Say: 15 rupees, 16 rupees, 20 rupees.
  3. How much? — Eighteen.
  4. Twenty — please reduce a little.
  5. Not 100 — 50.
