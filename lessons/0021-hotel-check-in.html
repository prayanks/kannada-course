Lesson 0021 Spoken Kannada ~12 min

Hotel check-in

Walk up to a lodge desk, claim a booking or ask for a vacant room, get the key, and close with check-out + bill.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Run the front-desk loop: Khali room ideya? → Ondu raatri beku → Nanna booking ide → Key kodri → Check-out yaavaaga? → Bill kodri

Spaced review (L20 + L3 + L9): Neevu ellinda? · Nanna ooru… · Eshtu? · Ondu / eradu · Eshtu gante? · Namaskara · Dhanyavadagalu

01 Why this matters

After hometown talk (L20), travel talk is next. At a Karnataka lodge or hotel desk you’ll hear heavy Kannada–English mix: room, booking, check-in, check-out, WiFi, key sit inside Kannada frames. One trap: on the street hotel often means a restaurant / meals place; for sleep people also say lodge or just room. Today you handle the desk without freezing.

02 One lever: ideya? + duration + desk loans

Pattern

… ideya? = is there / do you have…? (availability)
Khali room ideya? = is a vacant room available?
Ondu raatri / eradu raatri = one night / two nights
Nanna booking ide = I have a booking
Key kodri · Bill kodri = please give key / bill (same polite kodri as food L5)

Hindi bridge: Khali room hai? → Khali room ideya? · Ek raat ke liye → Ondu raatri-ge · Key dijiye → Key kodri.

03 Desk board (say these out loud)

room lodge khali booking key check-in check-out WiFi single double raatri baadige

Khali = empty / vacant. Raatri = night (stay length). Baadige = rent / room charge. Single/double are usually English at the desk.

05 Phrase cards

Khali room ideya?

slownatural

Is there a vacant room?

≈ Khali room hai kya?

ideya? = is there? Core availability ask. Also: Room ideya?

Ondu room beku · Ondu raatri beku

slownatural
slownatural

I need one room · I need one night

≈ Ek room chahiye · Ek raat chahiye

Same beku as food (L5). Swap eradu raatri for two nights.

Nanna booking ide · Nanna hesaru Prayank

slownatural
slownatural

I have a booking · My name is Prayank

≈ Meri booking hai · Mera naam Prayank

Name frame from L1. Staff often reply in English; your Kannada still opens the door.

Room eshtu? · Ondu raatri-ge eshtu?

slownatural
slownatural

How much is the room? · How much for one night?

≈ Room kitna? · Ek raat ka kitna?

-ge on duration = “for …”. Reuses L3 eshtu.

Key kodri · WiFi password heli

slownatural
slownatural

Please give the key · Tell me the WiFi password

≈ Key dijiye · WiFi password bataiye

kodri = give (polite). heli = tell (from repair talk L18 family).

Check-out yaavaaga? · Check-out eshtu gante?

slownatural
slownatural

When is check-out? · What time is check-out?

≈ Check-out kab hai? · Kitne baje check-out?

Clock frame from L9. Loanword check-out is normal desk speech.

Bill kodri · Room clean maadi

slownatural
slownatural

Please give the bill · Please clean the room

≈ Bill dijiye · Room saaf kar dijiye

Checkout close + housekeeping ask. maadi = do (polite).

07 Model dialogue — lodge front desk

You
Namaskara. Nanna booking ide. Nanna hesaru Prayank.

slownatural

Hello. I have a booking. My name is Prayank.

Reception
Sari. Ondu raatri na eradu?

slownatural

Okay. One night or two?

You
Ondu raatri beku. Room eshtu?

slownatural

One night. How much is the room?

Reception
Double room — eradu savira rupayi.

slownatural

Double room — two thousand rupees.

You
Sari. Key kodri. WiFi password heli.

slownatural

Okay. Please give the key. Tell me the WiFi password.

Reception
Idhu key. Check-out hattu gante.

slownatural

Here’s the key. Check-out is 10 o’clock.

You
Gothaytu. Dhanyavadagalu!

slownatural

Got it. Thank you!

08 Retrieval practice

1. “Is there a vacant room?”
2. “I need one night”
3. “I have a booking”
4. “Please give the key”
5. “When is check-out?”
6. Spaced (L3): “How much is this?”

09 Production

  1. Is there a vacant room?
  2. I need one night / two nights.
  3. I have a booking. My name is …
  4. How much for one night?
  5. Please give the key. WiFi password?
  6. When is check-out? Bill please.

Cover English; speak out loud. Repair with Matte heli / Nidhana heli (L18) if the desk replies fast.
