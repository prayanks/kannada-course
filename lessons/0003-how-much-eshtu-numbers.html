Lesson 0003 Spoken Kannada ~12 min

How much? (Eshtu + numbers)

Street skill: ask the price, catch numbers 1–10 (and a few money anchors), and push back politely — shops, stalls, and autos in Bengaluru.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

You can run: Idhu eshtu? → … rupayi → Swalpa kammi maadi → Sari / Beda and recognise ondu → hattu when you hear them.

Spaced review: Hegiddiri? · Chennagidini · Elli hogtiddiri? · Naanu manege hogtidini · Sari, nodona · Olle dinagalu

01 Why this matters

Neighbour chat gets you into the building. Eshtu? gets you through the city — fruit cart, medical store, auto meter arguments. This is the highest-ROI street phrase after hello.

02 One grammar lever: question word eshtu

Pattern

Eshtu? = how much / how many (≈ Hindi kitna/kitne?)
Point + ask: Idhu eshtu? = How much is this?
Ride: Alli eshtu? / … ge eshtu? = How much to there / to X?

Answers usually land as a number + rupayi (rupees). You don’t need perfect production of every number yet — first goal is hearing 1–10 and saying the price question confidently.

04 Phrase cards

Idhu eshtu?

slownatural

How much is this?

≈ Yeh kitna hai?

Word-by-word: idhu (this) · eshtu (how much) · Point with your hand/chin while asking — universal.

Adhu eshtu?

slownatural

How much is that?

≈ Woh kitna hai?

idhu = this (near) · adhu = that (far) — like yeh / woh.

… rupayi

… rupees

≈ … rupaye

You’ll hear: aidu rupayi (₹5), hattu rupayi (₹10), nooru rupayi (₹100). English “rupees” is also understood; locals still say rupayi a lot.

Swalpa kammi maadi

slownatural

Please reduce a little

≈ Thoda kam kijiye

swalpa = a little · kammi = less · maadi = do (polite). Soft bargain line for shops/autos — not aggressive.

Jaasti aytu

slownatural

That’s too much

≈ Zyada ho gaya

jaasti = more/too much · aytu ≈ it became / it is. Common pushback.

Sari · Beda

Okay · Don’t want / no need

≈ Theek · Nahi chahiye

Beda is the clean no. Soften with Namaskara + smile if walking away.

Meter haki · Fix a?

slownatural

Put the meter · Is it a fixed fare?

≈ Meter lagaao · Fix hai kya?

Bengaluru auto reality: mix of Kannada + English. Meter haki (put meter) is gold. You’ll also hear pure English numbers — still answer with Eshtu? first.

Alli eshtu? / Koramangala-ge eshtu?

slownatural
slownatural

How much to there? / How much to Koramangala?

≈ Wahaan kitna? / Koramangala kitna?

Reuse L2 -ge: place-ge + eshtu.

06 Model dialogues

A · Shop

You
Namaskara. Idhu eshtu?

slownatural

Hello. How much is this?

Shop
Hattu rupayi.

slownatural

Ten rupees.

You
Swalpa kammi maadi.

slownatural

Please reduce a little.

Shop
Entu rupayi sari.

slownatural

Eight rupees, okay.

You
Sari. Dhanyavadagalu.

slownatural

Okay. Thank you.

B · Auto

You
Indiranagar-ge eshtu?

slownatural

How much to Indiranagar?

Driver
Nooru.

slownatural

Hundred.

You
Jaasti aytu. Meter haki.

slownatural

Too much. Put the meter.

Driver
Sari, haki.

slownatural

Okay, I’ll put it.

You
Sari. Hogona.

slownatural

Okay. Let’s go.

Hogona ≈ let’s go (bonus exit into motion — related to L2 hogu).

07 Retrieval practice

1. “How much is this?”
2. Number for 5
3. Number for 10
4. Soft bargain: “Please reduce a little”
5. “That’s too much”
6. Clean “no / don’t want”
7. Spaced: “I’m going home” (L2)

08 Production

Cover the page. Say:

  1. Numbers 1→10 in order (ondu…hattu).
  2. How much is this?
  3. Please reduce a little.
  4. That’s too much. Put the meter.
  5. No need. Thank you.
  6. How much to Koramangala?

09 Real-world task

Before next lesson
  1. At one shop/stall: ask Idhu eshtu? (even if you pay the asked price).
  2. Drill numbers out loud once a day while walking — 1–10 forward and back.
  3. Optional auto: open with destination + eshtu? or Meter haki.
  4. Note any number word you heard and didn’t catch — send it to me.
