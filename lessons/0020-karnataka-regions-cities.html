Karnataka regions & cities

Ask someone’s hometown, name major cities, and place them on the state’s mental map — neighbour small talk gold.

Today’s win

Run the hometown loop: Neevu ellinda? → Nanna ooru Bengaluru → Nimma ooru elli? → Mysuru / Mangaluru / Hubballi…

Spaced review (L2 + L1): Elli hogtiddiri? · Manege / office-ge · Illi · Alli · Namaskara · Hegiddiri? · Nanna hesaru…

01 Why this matters

After “where are you going?” (L2), the next neighbour question is almost always where are you from? Locals use official city names (Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangaluru, Belagavi, Hubballi) more than old English spellings on the street. Karnataka has ~31 districts in four admin divisions — you don’t need the roster; you need hometown talk + a few region labels so “I’m from the coast / north” lands.

02 One lever: ooru + ellinda + place-inda

Pattern

Ooru = hometown / town / city (≈ “merā shahar / gaon” feeling — belonging, not just GPS)
Neevu ellinda? = where are you from? (polite)
Nanna ooru X = my place is X
X-inda = from X · Naanu Mysuru-inda = I’m from Mysuru
Yaava ooru? = which city/town?

Same politeness stack as always: default neevu / nimma with neighbours. Hindi bridge: Aap kahan se hain? → Neevu ellinda?

03 City board (say these out loud)

Bengaluru Mysuru Mangaluru Udupi Hubballi Belagavi Kalaburagi Kodagu

Capital Bengaluru. Palace/heritage hub Mysuru. Coast pair Mangaluru · Udupi. North commercial twin-city side Hubballi (often Hubballi–Dharwad). Northwest Belagavi. Northeast Kalaburagi (older English: Gulbarga). Hills/coffee district people often just say Kodagu (Coorg).

04 Region pocket map (spoken labels)

Karavali

Coast — Mangaluru, Udupi side. Fish, neer dosa country.

Malenadu

Western Ghats belt — rainy hills, coffee, Kodagu / Chikkamagaluru vibes.

Uttara Karnataka

North Karnataka — Belagavi, Hubballi, Kalaburagi belt in casual talk.

Mysuru / south side

Mysuru, Mandya, Hassan area people often say “Mysuru side” in mixed KN–EN.

Admin fact (for orientation only): districts sit under four divisions — Belagavi, Bengaluru, Kalaburagi, Mysuru. You won’t quiz that; the chips above are what people say.

05 Phrase cards

Neevu ellinda?

slownatural

Where are you from? (polite)

≈ Aap kahan se hain?

ellinda = from where. Core opener after names.

Nimma ooru elli?

slownatural

Where is your hometown / place?

≈ Aapka shehar / gaon kahan hai?

ooru carries belonging. Softer than listing a GPS pin.

Nanna ooru Bengaluru

slownatural

My place is Bengaluru

≈ Mera shehar Bengaluru hai

Swap any city: Nanna ooru Mysuru / Mangaluru / Hubballi.

Naanu Mysuru-inda · Naanu Mangaluru-inda

slownatural
slownatural

I’m from Mysuru · I’m from Mangaluru

≈ Main Mysuru se · Main Mangaluru se

-inda = from (ablative). Glue to any place name.

Yaava ooru?

slownatural

Which city / town?

≈ Kaunsa shehar?

Follow-up when someone says a region only (“north side”).

Karavali · Malenadu · Uttara Karnataka

slownatural

Coast · Malnad hills · North Karnataka

≈ Coastal / pahadi belt / uttar Karnataka

Region labels for “roughly where” before you pin a city.

Naanu Bengaluru-alli iddini · Illi kelasa

slownatural
slownatural

I live / am in Bengaluru · Work is here

≈ Main Bengaluru mein hoon · Yahan kaam

Separates hometown (ooru) from current base (alli / illi) — very common in Bengaluru.

08 Retrieval practice

1. “Where are you from?” (polite)
2. “My hometown is Mysuru”
3. “I’m from Mangaluru”
4. Coastal belt word
5. “Which town/city?”
6. Spaced (L2): “Where are you going?”

09 Production

  1. Where are you from? (polite)
  2. My place is Bengaluru.
  3. I’m from Hubballi.
  4. Coast — Mangaluru.
  5. Which town — Mysuru or Kodagu?
  6. I live / work here in Bengaluru.

Cover English; speak out loud. Repair with Matte heli / Nidhana heli (L18) if needed.
