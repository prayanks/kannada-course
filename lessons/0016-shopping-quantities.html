Lesson 0016 Spoken Kannada ~12 min

Shopping quantities

Kirana and vegetable-market amounts: half / full kilo, packets, pieces, “a little more,” and “this much is enough.”

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Order with Ardha kilo · Ondu kilo · Eradu packet · Ondu piece · Innu swalpa kodri · Idhe sari / Aaytu after *Idhu eshtu?*

Spaced review: Idhu eshtu? · Eradu kodri · Swalpa kammi maadi · Bill kodri · Ondu…ippatthu · Matte heli · Aaytu

01 Why this matters

You can ask the price and buy “two” at a counter. Real kirana talk is half kg tomatoes, one packet milk, two pieces. This lesson fills that hole without new grammar theory.

02 One lever: amount + item + kodri/beku

Pattern

[quantity] [item] kodri
Ardha kilo tomato kodri
Eradu packet milk beku
English item names are normal in Bengaluru shops.

04 Phrase cards

Ardha kilo kodri

slownatural

Half a kilo, please

≈ Aadha kilo dijiye

ardha you know from clock half — same word, weight sense here.

Ondu kilo · Eradu kilo

slownatural
slownatural

One kilo · Two kilos

≈ Ek kilo · Do kilo

Number + kilo. Add item: Ondu kilo onion kodri.

Ondu packet · Eradu packet

slownatural
slownatural

One packet · Two packets

≈ Ek packet · Do packet

Milk, bread, snacks. EN packet is the shop default.

Ondu piece · Mooru number

slownatural
slownatural

One piece · Three pieces (count items)

≈ Ek piece · Teen number

number = countable pieces in shop Kannada-English mix (bananas, eggs trays sometimes).

Innu swalpa kodri

slownatural

Give a little more

≈ Thoda aur dijiye

innu (still/more) + swalpa + kodri. Classic weighing-scale line.

Idhe sari · Bas · Aaytu

slownatural

This much is enough · That’s all · Done

≈ Itna kaafi · Bas · Ho gaya

Stop the scoop. Bas is shared Hindi/Kannada shop talk.

Swalpa kammi maadi · Weight kammi

slownatural
slownatural

A little less · Reduce the weight

≈ Thoda kam · Weight kam

Recycle bargain kammi maadi onto quantity, not only price.

Fresh ide na? · Chennagi ide na?

slownatural
slownatural

Is it fresh? · Is it good?

≈ Fresh hai na? · Accha hai na?

Quality check before you commit weight.

Olle samanya · Khaali packet

slownatural
slownatural

Normal / regular one · Loose (not branded) packet

≈ Normal wala · Khula

Optional recognition. EN “loose / company” also heard — don’t force.

06 Model dialogue — vegetable stall

You
Namaskara. Tomato eshtu?

slownatural

Hello. How much are tomatoes?

Vendor
Hattu rupayi kilo.

slownatural

Ten rupees a kilo.

You
Ardha kilo kodri. Fresh ide na?

slownatural

Half kilo please. Fresh?

Vendor
Haudu. Nodri.

slownatural

Yes. Look.

You
Innu swalpa kodri… Sari, idhe sari. Onion ondu kilo kodri.

slownatural

A little more… Okay, enough. One kilo onion.

Vendor
Aaytu. Bera eshtu aytu — hadinaidu.

slownatural

Done. Total how much — fifteen.

You
Sari. Dhanyavadagalu.

slownatural

Okay. Thank you.

Bera / total may come as English “total.” Hadinaidu = 15 (L8).

07 Second micro-dialogue — kirana

You
Milk eradu packet beku. Bread ondu.

slownatural

Two milk packets. One bread.

Shop
Sari… Ippatthu rupayi.

slownatural

Okay… twenty rupees.

You
Eggs — aaru number kodri. Aaytu. Bill kodri.

slownatural

Eggs — six pieces. Done. Bill please.

  3. One kilo onion.
  4. Two milk packets.
  5. Six eggs (six number).
  6. Done. Bill please. How much total?
