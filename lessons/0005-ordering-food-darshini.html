Lesson 0005 Spoken Kannada ~11 min

Ordering food (darshini / cafe)

Order tea, coffee, and a simple plate; say how many; ask for the bill — Bengaluru counter culture, Roman only.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Run a full counter loop: Namaskara → … kodri / beku → … eshtu? → Bill kodri → Dhanyavadagalu with tea/coffee and one food item.

Spaced review: Idhu eshtu? · ondu…hattu · balakke / yedakke / eduru · elli ide? · Swalpa kammi maadi · Beda

01 Why this matters

Darshinis and filter-coffee counters are daily Bengaluru. You already have prices (L3) and politeness (L1). This lesson glues them into ordering — the most repeated public script after auto talk.

02 One grammar lever: beku / beda + polite kodri

Pattern

beku = want / need (≈ chahiye)
beda = don’t want (you know from L3)
kodri = please give (polite; ≈ dijiye)
Order shape: [item] [number] kodri or [item] beku

Examples: Coffee ondu kodri · Idli eradu beku · Bill kodri.

03 High-frequency items (recognition + production)

coffee / kaafi tea / cha filter coffee idli vada dosa upma poori meals / oota water / neeru

English item names are normal at counters. Still learn neeru (water) and oota (meal/food).

04 Phrase cards

Coffee ondu kodri

slownatural

One coffee, please

≈ Ek coffee dijiye

ondu = one (L3). Swap: tea, filter coffee, water.

Idli eradu beku

slownatural

I want two idlis

≈ Mujhe do idli chahiye

beku is direct and common at counters — not rude in this context.

Masala dosa ondu kodri

slownatural

One masala dosa, please

≈ Ek masala dosa dijiye

Food name stays English/Kannada hybrid — that’s authentic.

Neeru kodri

slownatural

Water, please

≈ Paani dijiye

neeru = water. Also hear: “water bottle”.

Swalpa less sweet · Strong maadi

slownatural
slownatural

A little less sweet · Make it strong

≈ Thodi kam meethi · Strong banana

Coffee/tea tweaks. Reuses swalpa (L3). Mix of EN + KN is normal.

Parcel maadi · Iggade

slownatural

Pack it / parcel · Here (for here)

≈ Parcel kar do · Yahi pe

Parcel maadi = pack. Iggade ≈ here (for sitting/eating here). You’ll also hear plain “parcel” / “here only”.

Bill kodri · Eshtu aytu?

slownatural
slownatural

Bill please · How much did it come to?

≈ Bill dijiye · Kitna hua?

Combines L3 eshtu with bill close. aytu you saw in jaasti aytu.

Innu eradu nimisha · Gottu

slownatural

Two more minutes · Okay/understood

≈ Do minute aur · Theek

Optional wait language. innu ≈ more/still. Gottu ≈ okay (related to gothaytu).

06 Model dialogue — darshini counter

You
Namaskara. Filter coffee ondu kodri. Idli eradu beku.

slownatural

Hello. One filter coffee please. Two idlis.

Counter
Iggade na? Parcel na?

slownatural

Here or parcel?

You
Iggade. Neeru kodri.

slownatural

Here. Water please.

Counter
Sari… Coffee ready.

slownatural

Okay… coffee ready.

You
Bill kodri. Eshtu aytu?

slownatural

Bill please. How much?

Counter
Aivattu rupayi.

slownatural

Fifty rupees.

You
Sari. Dhanyavadagalu.

slownatural

Okay. Thank you.

07 Retrieval practice

1. Polite “please give”
2. “I want / need”
3. “One coffee, please”
4. Water
5. “Bill please”
6. Pack it / takeaway
7. Spaced: number 2 (L3)

08 Production

  1. One filter coffee, please.
  2. Two idlis. Here (not parcel).
  3. Water, please.
  4. Pack the dosa.
  5. Bill please. How much is it?
  6. No need (if offered extra). Thank you.
