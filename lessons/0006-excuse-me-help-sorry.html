Lesson 0006 Spoken Kannada ~10 min

Excuse me, help, sorry

Survival politeness for crowded Bengaluru: get attention, ask for help, apologise, ask someone to wait — without freezing into English only.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

You can use Kshamisi · Dayavittu · Sahaya maadi · Swalpa wait maadi · Tumba thanks in real micro-moments (queue, bump, ask a stranger).

Spaced review: Coffee ondu kodri · Bill kodri · elli ide? · balakke · Idhu eshtu? · Gothaytu · Dhanyavadagalu

01 Why this matters

You’ve covered scripts (greet, go, price, path, food). Real life is messy interruptions. These phrases are the glue between scripts — shops, metro, society lobby, wrong turn.

02 One lever: maadi = polite “please do”

Pattern

You already used maadi in swalpa kammi maadi and parcel maadi.
Stack it: [request] maadi ≈ please do X.
Softener: dayavittu ≈ please / kindly (≈ kripya).

03 Phrase cards

Kshamisi

Excuse me / sorry (getting attention or mild apology)

≈ Maaf kijiye / excuse me

Default opener before asking a stranger. Also after a small bump.

Dayavittu

Please / kindly

≈ Kripya

Put before a request: Dayavittu, elli ide?

Sahaya maadi

slownatural

Please help

≈ Madad kijiye

sahaya = help. Full: Dayavittu sahaya maadi.

Swalpa wait maadi · Innu ondu nimisha

slownatural
slownatural

Please wait a bit · One more minute

≈ Thoda wait kijiye · Ek minute aur

Reuses swalpa, innu, ondu. EN “wait” is fully normal in Bengaluru Kannada mix.

Tumba sorry · Kshamisi maadi

slownatural
slownatural

So sorry · Please forgive me

≈ Bahut sorry · Maaf kardo

tumba = very/a lot. Stronger apology than plain kshamisi.

Tumba dhanyavadagalu · Tumba thanks

slownatural
slownatural

Thank you very much

≈ Bahut dhanyavaad

Upgrade from L1 thanks. Mix with English “thanks” is natural.

Gothilla / Gottila · Nanage gothilla

slownatural

I don’t know

≈ Mujhe nahi pata

You met gottila in L4. Nanage ≈ to me (like mujhe).

Kannada swalpa swalpa barutte · English OK?

slownatural
slownatural

I know a little Kannada · English OK?

≈ Thodi Kannada aati hai

Honesty line when stuck. barutte ≈ comes (it comes to me). People usually switch or slow down — that’s a win.

Matte sigona · Sari hogi

slownatural
slownatural

See you again · Okay, go ahead

≈ Phir milte hain · Theek, jaao

Soft closers. matte ≈ again. Related to L2 nodona energy.

05 Model dialogues

A · Ask a stranger

You
Kshamisi. Dayavittu, metro gate elli ide?

slownatural

Excuse me. Please, where is the metro gate?

Stranger
Eduru hogi…

slownatural

Go straight…

You
Gothaytu. Tumba dhanyavadagalu.

slownatural

Got it. Thank you very much.

B · Bump + help

You
Tumba sorry! Kshamisi.

slownatural

So sorry! Excuse me.

You
Dayavittu sahaya maadi — bag beelitu.

slownatural

Please help — the bag fell. (beelitu ≈ fell; bonus recognition)

Other
Sari sari.

slownatural

It’s fine.

You
Tumba thanks.

slownatural

Thanks a lot.

06 Retrieval practice

1. “Excuse me / sorry” (default)
2. “Please / kindly”
3. “Please help”
4. “Very / a lot”
5. “I don’t know”
6. Spaced: “please give” at counter (L5)

07 Production

  1. Excuse me. Please, where is the gate?
  2. Please help.
  3. Please wait a bit.
  4. So sorry.
  5. Thank you very much.
  6. I don’t know. I know a little Kannada.

Before next lesson

  3. If stuck mid-conversation: Kannada swalpa swalpa barutte.
