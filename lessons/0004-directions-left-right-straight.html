Lesson 0004 Spoken Kannada ~10 min

Directions (left, right, straight)

Ask where a place is, catch the three core direction words, and confirm before you walk or tell an auto — still Roman only.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

You can ask … elli ide? / Heghe hogbeku?, understand eduru · balakke · yedakke, and check with Idhe na? / Alli na?

Spaced review: Elli hogtiddiri? · manege · Idhu eshtu? · Swalpa kammi maadi · ondu eradu mooru … hattu · Meter haki

01 Why this matters

You already ask where someone is going (L2) and how much (L3). Directions close the loop: society gate, shop inside a complex, “which way to the clubhouse?” Three words unlock most answers — left, right, straight.

02 One grammar lever: elli ide? = where is it?

Pattern

Elli? = where? (you know this)
Ide / ideya? ≈ is / is it? (existence)
Clubhouse elli ide? ≈ Where is the clubhouse?
Hindi bridge: Clubhouse kahaan hai?

Path question: Heghe hogbeku? ≈ How should I go? / Which way? (You’ll also hear English “which way?” — answer words below still matter.)

03 The three direction words

↑ eduru / nera
straight ahead
→ balakke
to the right
← yedakke
to the left

Memory hook (Hindi): bal-akkе feels like stronger/right side for many learners; yeda = left. You’ll also hear right / left / straight in English mixed in — still learn Kannada so you catch pure answers.

Variants: balagade / yedagade, nedage (straight). Default production: balakke · yedakke · eduru.

04 Phrase cards

… elli ide?

slownatural

Where is …?

≈ … kahaan hai?

Examples: Restroom elli ide? · Gate elli ide? · Medical elli ide?

Heghe hogbeku?

slownatural

Which way should I go? / How do I go?

≈ Kaise jaana hai? / Kidhar se?

heghe ≈ how/which way · hogbeku ≈ must go / should go.

Eduru hogi

slownatural

Go straight

≈ Seedha jaao

Polite-ish instruction form you’ll hear. Also: Eduru hogi ri / just Straight hogi.

Balakke thirgi

slownatural

Turn right

≈ Right mudo / dayein mudiye

thirgi ≈ turn. Pair: Yedakke thirgi = turn left.

Swalpa eduru, nantara balakke

slownatural

A little straight, then right

≈ Thoda seedha, phir right

Reuses L3 swalpa. nantara ≈ then/after that.

Hattira / Dooradalli

Nearby / Far

≈ Paas mein / Door

Distance glue when someone answers without a turn.

Idhe na? · Alli na?

slownatural
slownatural

Is it this? · Is it there?

≈ Yeh wala? · Wahaan?

Confirmation tag na? ≈ right?/no? — very Bengaluru. Point while asking.

Gothaytu · Gottila

Got it / I understand · I don’t know

≈ Samajh gaya · Pata nahi

Gothaytu when directions land. Gottila if you’re the one asked and don’t know — honest and common.

06 Model dialogue

You
Namaskara. Clubhouse elli ide?

slownatural

Hello. Where is the clubhouse?

Guard
Eduru hogi. Nantara balakke.

slownatural

Go straight. Then right.

You
Swalpa eduru, nantara balakke — idhe na?

slownatural

A little straight, then right — this way?

Guard
Haudu. Hattira ide.

slownatural

Yes. It’s nearby.

You
Gothaytu. Dhanyavadagalu.

slownatural

Got it. Thank you.

Haudu ≈ yes (bonus; you’ll also hear yes / sari).

07 Retrieval practice

1. “Where is the gate?”
2. “To the right”
3. “To the left”
4. “Go straight”
5. “Got it / I understand”
6. Spaced: “How much is this?” (L3)

08 Production

  1. Where is the clubhouse?
  2. Which way should I go?
  3. Go straight. Then turn right.
  4. Turn left.
  5. Is it this way? It’s nearby.
  6. Got it. Thank you. / I don’t know.

09 Real-world task

Before next lesson
  1. Ask one person: … elli ide? (clubhouse, waste room, exit, medical — anything real).
  2. Repeat their answer back with direction words: eduru / balakke / yedakke.
  3. Confirm once: Idhe na? then Gothaytu, dhanyavadagalu.
