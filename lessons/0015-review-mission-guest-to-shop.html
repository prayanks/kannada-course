Lesson 0015 · Review Spoken Kannada ~15 min

Review mission: guest → gate → auto → shop

One continuous Bengaluru afternoon. Almost no new grammar — just retrieve and chain what you already own.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Run the full chain out loud without notes: tell security a guest is coming → greet them → auto to market → buy something → back home.

Only 4 light glue words (optional new): Aaytu (done / finished) · Barthini (I’m coming) · Tumba busy · Matte heli (say again)

01 Why a review lesson

Lessons 1–14 spread a lot of surface area. Speaking fluency comes from recombining old cards under time pressure, not only collecting new ones. This mission is your spaced-repetition “final boss” for unit one.

03 New / refreshed glue only

Aaytu

Done / finished / over

≈ Ho gaya

You’ve seen related aytu. Use after a task: Aaytu = done.

Barthini

I’m coming

≈ Main aa raha/rahi hoon

Pairs with L11 barthidare (they’re coming). Phone: “Barthini — ondu nimisha.”

Tumba busy

slownatural

Very busy

≈ Bahut busy

EN busy + tumba. Soft refuse or explain delay.

Matte heli

slownatural

Say it again

≈ Phir se boliye

Repair phrase when you don’t catch speech. Optional: Nidhana heli (say slowly) later.

You

slownatural

You

slownatural

Shop

05 Retrieval blitz

1. Tell gate a guest is coming
2. Polite “how are you?”
3. “Let’s go to market + meter”
4. Stop the auto here
5. “Two please” at counter
6. New glue: “say again”
7. New glue: “I’m coming”
8. “Done / finished”

06 Production (cover everything)

Without scrolling up, speak the mission in 8 beats:

  1. Call gate: name, flat, guest coming, call me.
  2. Greet guest + how are you + come in.
  3. Comment on heat.
  4. Offer coffee.
  5. Auto: market, meter, stop here, how much?
  6. Shop: how much, two please, reduce a little, bill.
  7. Say done. Going home.
  8. I’m coming — ten minutes. Thank you.
