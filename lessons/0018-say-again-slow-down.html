Lesson 0018 Spoken Kannada ~11 min

Say again, slow down (repair talk)

Stay in the conversation when speech is fast — the meta-skill that unlocks every future lesson.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Smoothly use Matte heli · Nidhana heli · Swalpa slow · Gotthilla · English nalli heli · Kannada swalpa swalpa barutte without freezing into full English panic.

Spaced review: Kshamisi · Dayavittu · Matte heli (L15) · Nanage gothilla · Kannada swalpa swalpa barutte (L6)

01 Why this matters

You already generate shop, auto, and society lines. Real locals speak faster than lessons. Repair talk keeps the channel open: ask again, slow down, admit limits, switch lanes politely.

02 One lever: heli (say / tell)

Pattern

Matte heli = say again
Nidhana heli = say slowly
English nalli heli = say it in English
Stack with dayavittu / kshamisi for softness.

slownatural

06 Retrieval practice

1. “Say again”
2. “Say slowly”
3. “I didn’t understand”
4. “Say it in English”
5. Close the loop: “got it”
6. Spaced: “I know a little Kannada” (L6)

07 Production

  1. Sorry — say again. Slowly.
  2. I didn’t understand.
  3. Please say it in English.
  4. I know a little Kannada.
  5. Wait one minute.
  6. Okay, got it. One fifty, you said?

08 Real-world task

This week
  1. Use Matte heli or Nidhana heli once with a real human (shop, guard, auto).
  2. Close with Sari, gothaytu so they know you’re tracking.
  3. If still lost: English nalli heli — then retry one Kannada word on the way out.
