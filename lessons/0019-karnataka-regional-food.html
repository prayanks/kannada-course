Lesson 0019 Spoken Kannada ~12 min

Karnataka regional food

Order state specialties beyond idli-dosa — ask what’s special, say what you like, tune the spice.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

At a Karnataka meals / udupi-style counter, run: Yen special ide? → [dish] ondu kodri → Khara kammi · Nanage tumba ishta → Bill kodri

Spaced review (L5): … kodri · … beku · Parcel maadi · Iggade · Neeru kodri · Bill kodri · Eshtu aytu?

01 Why this matters

L5 got you through tea, idli, and the bill. Locals and hotel boards push bisi bele bath, ragi mudde, neer dosa, akki rotti, vangi bath, mysore pak. You need dish names + one ask (“what’s special?”) + one taste line (“I like it”) so you don’t freeze into English-only.

02 One lever: ishta + yen special ide?

Pattern

Yen special ide? = what’s the special / recommendation?
Nanage … ishta = I like … (≈ mujhe … pasand)
Tumba ishta = like it a lot
Order still uses L5: [dish] ondu kodri / [dish] beku

Bisi here is the dish word “hot” (as in hot lentil rice) — same root you heard for weather heat in L12, different context.

03 Regional plate board (recognition)

bisi bele bath ragi mudde neer dosa akki rotti vangi bath mysore pak saaru khara

Bisi bele bath = hot lentil-rice one-pot. Ragi mudde = finger-millet balls (often with saaru). Neer dosa = thin rice crepe. Akki rotti = rice flatbread. Vangi bath = brinjal rice. Mysore pak = ghee-besan sweet. Dish names stay hybrid EN/KN on boards — that’s normal.

04 Phrase cards

Yen special ide?

slownatural

What’s special? / What do you recommend?

≈ Kya special hai?

yen = what (spoken). ide = is/there is. Opens the counter chat.

Bisi bele bath ondu kodri

slownatural

One bisi bele bath, please

≈ Ek bisi bele bath dijiye

L5 order frame. Swap any dish name in the same slot.

Ragi mudde matte saaru beku

slownatural

I want ragi mudde and saaru

≈ Ragi mudde aur saaru chahiye

matte ≈ and/also (you know from repair “matte heli”). saaru = thin spiced gravy (often with mudde).

Neer dosa eradu kodri · Akki rotti ondu beku

slownatural
slownatural

Two neer dosas please · One akki rotti

≈ Do neer dosa · Ek akki rotti

Numbers from L3. Same counter muscle as idli/dosa.

Khara kammi maadi · Swalpa less spicy

slownatural
slownatural

Make it less spicy · A bit less spicy

≈ Teekha kam karo

khara = spicy/hot (chilli heat, not temperature). Mix KN + EN is native Bengaluru.

Nanage tumba ishta · Olledu

slownatural

I like it a lot · It’s good / nice

≈ Mujhe bahut pasand · Achha hai

Taste feedback to staff or friends. olledu ≈ good thing / nice.

Vangi bath parcel maadi · Mysore pak ondu kodri

slownatural
slownatural

Pack vangi bath · One mysore pak please

≈ Vangi bath parcel · Ek mysore pak

Sweet close + L5 parcel. Great takeaway stack.

06 Model dialogue — meals counter

You
Namaskara. Yen special ide?

slownatural

Hello. What’s special?

Counter
Bisi bele bath, ragi mudde, neer dosa — ella ide.

slownatural

Bisi bele bath, ragi mudde, neer dosa — all are there.

You
Bisi bele bath ondu kodri. Khara kammi maadi.

slownatural

One bisi bele bath please. Less spicy.

Counter
Sari. Iggade na parcel?

slownatural

Okay. Here or parcel?

You
Iggade. Mysore pak ondu kodri.

slownatural

Here. One mysore pak please.

You (after tasting)
Olledu — nanage tumba ishta. Bill kodri.

slownatural

Nice — I like it a lot. Bill please.

07 Retrieval practice

1. “What’s special?”
2. One bisi bele bath, please
3. “Less spicy”
4. “I like it a lot”
5. Ragi mudde + gravy order
6. Spaced (L5): “Bill please”

08 Production

  1. What’s special today?
  2. One bisi bele bath, less spicy.
  3. Ragi mudde and saaru.
  4. Two neer dosas + one mysore pak parcel.
  5. It’s good — I like it a lot. Bill please.

Cover English; speak out loud. If stuck, repair with Matte heli / Nidhana heli (L18).

09 Real-world task

This week
  1. At any Karnataka / Udupi / meals place, ask Yen special ide? once (even if you already know).
  2. Order one regional plate from this lesson with … kodri or … beku.
  3. Close with taste line: Olledu or Nanage tumba ishta — then Bill kodri.
