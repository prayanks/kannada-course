Lesson 0011 Spoken Kannada ~11 min

Society staff (guard, delivery, help)

Gate, parcel, and flat talk with the people you see every day — stitch L1 politeness + L4 place + L10 phone into one society script.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

At the gate you can say who you are / who you’re visiting, handle a parcel, and ask staff to call you — Nanna flat … · Parcel ide · Nanage kare maadi · Mele hogi.

Spaced review: Namaskara · Kshamisi · Elli ide? · Nanage kare maadi · Ondu nimisha kaayri · Flat / gate words in English are fine

01 Why this matters

Your mission started with neighbours. The highest-frequency speakers in a Bengaluru society are often security, delivery, house staff. Short polite Kannada here builds real goodwill fast.

02 One lever: ide / illa + place words

Pattern

ide = is there / it is (L4)
illa = isn’t / not there (L10 energy)
Nanna flat-ge = to my flat
Mele / kelage = up / down (floors, lift)
