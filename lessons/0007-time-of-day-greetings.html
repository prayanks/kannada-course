Lesson 0007 Spoken Kannada ~11 min

Time-of-day greetings

Upgrade the all-day Namaskara with morning-to-night greetings, day words (today / tomorrow), and the classic Kannada goodbye — still Roman only.

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

Match the clock: Shubhodaya · Shubha madhyahna · Shubha sayankala · Shubharatri, say Ivattu / Naale, and close with Hogi banni or Shubha dinavagali.

Spaced review: Namaskara · Hegiddiri? · Kshamisi · Dayavittu · Olle dinagalu · Matte sigona · Gothaytu

01 Why this matters

In Bengaluru corridors, Namaskara works all day — keep it as your safe default. Time-of-day greetings add warmth when you meet the same neighbour at 7am vs 9pm, and they show up on TV, SMS festive lines, and more formal speech. Day words (ivattu / naale) unlock “see you tomorrow” small talk after L2’s nodona.

02 One lever: shubha- + time slot

Pattern

Shubha ≈ good / auspicious (like Hindi shubh).
Stack: Shubha + [time] → good morning / evening…
Exception shape you’ll hear as one word: Shubhodaya (morning), Shubharatri (night).

Hindi bridge: suprabhat / shubh din / shubh sandhya / shubh ratri — same social job, different clothes.

04 Phrase cards

Shubhodaya

Good morning

≈ Suprabhat / good morning

Slot: beligge (morning) · Safe with neighbours before ~11ish. Pair: Shubhodaya. Hegiddiri?

Shubha madhyahna

slownatural

Good afternoon

≈ Shubh dopahar

madhyahna = midday / afternoon. Less common than morning/evening in casual chat — still useful to recognise.

Shubha sayankala

slownatural

Good evening

≈ Shubh sandhya / good evening

sayankala = evening. Very usable after work in the society lift.

Shubharatri

Good night

≈ Shubh ratri

Closing line more than a first hello. After a late chat: Sari, shubharatri.

Ivattu · Naale · Ninne

Today · Tomorrow · Yesterday

≈ Aaj · Kal (aane wala) · Kal (guzra)

Production priority: ivattu + naale. Ninne = yesterday — recognition first.

Naale nodona

slownatural

See you tomorrow

≈ Kal milte hain

Reuses L2 nodona. Soft neighbour exit after evening walk.

Hogi banni

slownatural

Goodbye (lit. go and come)

≈ Jaake aana / alvida (warmer than cold bye)

Classic Kannada parting — implies “go safely and return.” Also hear: Hogi barteera? Also fine: English bye + Namaskara.

Shubha dinavagali · Olleyadagali

slownatural

Have a good day · All the best / may it be good

≈ Accha din ho · shubh ho

Upgrade path from L2 Olle dinagalu. Olleyadagali ≈ “may good things happen” — soft luck/closing wish.

Yaavaaga?

slownatural

When?

≈ Kab?

Bonus question word. Answer with ivattu / naale / sayankala for now — clock times come later.

06 Model dialogues

A · Morning neighbour

You
Shubhodaya. Hegiddiri?

slownatural

Good morning. How are you?

Neighbour
Chennagidini. Neevu?

slownatural

I’m fine. And you?

You
Chennagidini. Sari, hogi banni. Shubha dinavagali.

slownatural

I’m fine. Okay, bye. Have a good day.

B · Evening close

You
Namaskara. Shubha sayankala.

slownatural

Hello. Good evening.

Neighbour
Shubha sayankala. Elli hogtiddiri?

slownatural

Good evening. Where are you going?

You
Naanu manege hogtidini. Naale nodona.

slownatural

I’m going home. See you tomorrow.

Neighbour
Sari. Shubharatri.

slownatural

Okay. Good night.

07 Retrieval practice

1. “Good morning”
2. “Good evening”
3. “Tomorrow”
4. “See you tomorrow”
5. Classic Kannada goodbye (“go and come”)
6. Spaced: polite “How are you?” (L1)

08 Production

  1. Good morning. How are you?
  2. Good evening.
  3. I’m going home. See you tomorrow.
  4. Okay, goodbye. Have a good day.
  5. Good night.
  6. When? → Tomorrow. / Today.

09 Real-world task

Before next lesson
  1. One morning open: Shubhodaya (or Namaskara + Shubhodaya) with someone in your building.
  2. One evening close: Naale nodona or Hogi banni.
  3. Note whether they answered with a time greeting or plain Namaskara — both are data.
