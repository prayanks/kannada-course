Lesson 0012 Spoken Kannada ~10 min

Weather & small talk

Corridor and lift filler after hello — rain, heat, traffic — so you aren’t stuck after Hegiddiri?

Audio: Machine-generated Kannada (edge-tts · kn-IN-SapnaNeural). Helpful for rhythm and recognition — may not perfectly match native Bengaluru pronunciation. Prefer what you hear from neighbours when the two differ.
Today’s win

After a greeting you can add Ivattu tumba bisi · Male bartha ide · Traffic jaasti · Sari na? and keep the chat alive for 10 more seconds.

Spaced review: Hegiddiri? · Chennagidini · Ivattu · Tumba · Jaasti · Sari, nodona · Olle dinagalu

01 Why this matters

Neighbour talk dies without a second beat. Weather and traffic are safe, endless topics in Bengaluru — same social job as Hindi aaj bahut garmi / barish / traffic.

02 One lever: ide / bartha ide for “it’s …-ing”

Pattern

Bisi ide ≈ it is hot
Male bartha ide ≈ rain is coming / it’s raining
Tag agreement: Sari na? · Illa na?

04 Phrase cards

Ivattu tumba bisi

slownatural

Today is very hot

≈ Aaj bahut garmi hai

ivattu (L7) + tumba (L6) + bisi = hot.

Male bartha ide · Male bandide

slownatural
slownatural

It’s raining / rain is coming · Rain has come / started

≈ Baarish ho rahi hai

male = rain. You’ll also hear plain English “raining.”

Hottu jaasti · Mabbu ide

slownatural
slownatural

Sun is strong · It’s cloudy

≈ Dhoop zyada · Baadal hai

hottu ≈ sun/heat of sun · mabbu = clouds.

Swalpa thandi ide · Chali bartha ide

slownatural
slownatural

It’s a bit cool · Cold is coming (cooler air)

≈ Thodi thand hai

Bengaluru “cold” is relative. EN thandi is widely used too.

Traffic jaasti · Road block ide

slownatural
slownatural

Traffic is heavy · There’s a road block

≈ Traffic zyada hai

English nouns + Kannada glue — authentic city speech.

Olle climate ivattu · Fresh ide

slownatural
slownatural

Nice weather today · It’s fresh/pleasant

≈ Aaj mausam accha hai

Positive small talk after rain or winter mornings.

Umba / Sari na? · Haudu sari

slownatural
slownatural

Isn’t it? / Right? · Yes, right

≈ Hai na? · Haan theek

Agreement tags keep the ball in play. Sari na? is enough.

Nantara male barabahudu · Umbrella beku

slownatural
slownatural

It might rain later · Need an umbrella

≈ Baad mein baarish ho sakti hai

barabahudu ≈ may come. EN umbrella is fine.

06 Model dialogues

A · Lift / corridor

You
Namaskara. Hegiddiri?

slownatural

Hello. How are you?

Neighbour
Chennagidini. Neevu?

slownatural

I’m fine. You?

You
Chennagidini. Ivattu tumba bisi, sari na?

slownatural

I’m fine. Today is very hot, right?

Neighbour
Haudu. Male bartha ide anta.

slownatural

Yes. Looks like rain is coming.

You
Sari. Olle dinagalu.

slownatural

Okay. Have a good day.

B · Gate after rain

You
Male bandide! Traffic jaasti.

slownatural

Rain has started! Traffic is heavy.

Guard
Haudu sir. Careful.

slownatural

Yes sir. Careful.

You
Sari. Dhanyavadagalu.

slownatural

Okay. Thank you.

  3. Sun is strong.
  4. Traffic is heavy.
  5. Nice weather today.
  6. Might rain later — need umbrella.
