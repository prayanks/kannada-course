Karnataka regional food

Pocket sheet for Lesson 0019. Pattern: Yen special ide? → dish + kodri/beku → ishta / khara.

Order loop

  1. Namaskara. Yen special ide?
  2. Bisi bele bath ondu kodri (or ragi mudde / neer dosa / akki rotti / vangi bath)
  3. Khara kammi maadi (if needed)
  4. Iggade or Parcel maadi
  5. Mysore pak ondu kodri (optional sweet)
  6. Olledu — nanage tumba ishta. Bill kodri.
