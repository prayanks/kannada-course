Clock time

Pocket sheet for Lesson 0009. Pattern: number + gante (+ aytu / -ge).

  1. Eshtu gante aytu? — what time is it?
  2. Aidu gante aytu — it’s 5:00
  3. Hattu gante-ge banni — come at 10
  4. Naale elu gante-ge — tomorrow at 7
  5. Aidu gante ardha — 5:30
  6. Eega — now
  7. Tumba late aytu — very late
  8. Yaavaaga meeting? — when is the meeting?
