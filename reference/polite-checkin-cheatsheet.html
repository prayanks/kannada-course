Polite neighbour check-in

Pocket sheet for Lesson 0001. Roman only. Default register: neevu / nimma.

Core loop

  1. Namaskara — hello
  2. Hegiddiri? — how are you? (polite)
  3. Chennagidini — I’m fine
  4. Neevu? — and you?
  5. Nimma hesaru enu? — what’s your name?
  6. Nanna hesaru … — my name is …
  7. Dhanyavadagalu — thank you
  8. Sari — okay
