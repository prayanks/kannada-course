Phone call

Pocket sheet for Lesson 0010. Pattern: kare / phone + maadi.

  1. Halo — hello (phone)
  2. Nanage kare maadi — call me
  3. Naanu phone maadtini — I’ll call
  4. Ondu nimisha kaayri — wait one minute
  5. Yaaru maatadtiddira? — who’s speaking?
  6. Nanna hesaru … — my name is …
  7. Avaru eega illa — they’re not here now
  8. Tappa number — wrong number
  9. Line busy aagide — line is busy
  10. Nimma phone number eshtu? — what’s your number?
  11. Nantara nanage kare maadi — call me later
