Time-of-day greetings

Pocket sheet for Lesson 0007. Namaskara still works all day.

  1. Shubhodaya — good morning
  2. Shubha madhyahna — good afternoon
  3. Shubha sayankala — good evening
  4. Shubharatri — good night
  5. Ivattu / Naale / Ninne — today / tomorrow / yesterday
  6. Naale nodona — see you tomorrow
  7. Hogi banni — goodbye (go and come)
  8. Shubha dinavagali — have a good day
  9. Olleyadagali — all the best
  10. Yaavaaga? — when?
