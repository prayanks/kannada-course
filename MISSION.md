# Mission: Spoken Kannada for Bengaluru life

## Why
You live in Bengaluru and want to talk comfortably with neighbours and locals — greetings, small talk, shops, autos — without reading or writing the script. Success is being understood and understanding replies in everyday spoken Kannada.

## Success looks like
- Greet a neighbour politely and complete a short check-in exchange
- Handle basic shopping / auto price talk (“how much?”)
- Follow simple directions and common replies
- Sound natural in Bengaluru spoken style (not textbook-only)

## Constraints
- Languages already known: Hindi, English — use Hindi SOV + politeness parallels heavily
- **Roman transliteration only** (no Kannada script)
- Speak + understand first; reading/writing out of scope for now
- Short lessons: ≤8–12 new words, one grammar pattern, dialogue + production
- Prefer spaced review of older vocab

## Out of scope
- Kannada script / literacy
- Formal literary Kannada
- Deep grammar theory without speaking payoff
